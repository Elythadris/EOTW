# QAnon archive: the October 2017 extradition claim

The JSON-QAnon research corpus catalogs an anonymous post as drop 1, original 4chan /pol/ post **147012719**, dated **October 28, 2017, 20:44 UTC**. It followed a separate anonymous post that predicted Hillary Clinton’s arrest. Drop 1 opened with the claim “HRC extradition already in motion”. This record summarizes the historical post and its provenance.

- **Archived evidence:** The corpus supplies the post text, timestamp, board, and original post ID. The URL in the corpus metadata points to a preserved copy of the anonymous thread. The corpus aggregates historical copies. Neither source establishes the author’s identity, access, or the truth of the post.
- **Contrary evidence:** Chapter 6 of the House Select Committee’s report records that the predicted arrest did not occur.
- **Open question:** The archived text alone cannot establish who wrote it, what information they had, or why they wrote it. Check the post identifier and the archived thread when comparing versions. Look for independent contemporaneous records when assessing claims about government action.
