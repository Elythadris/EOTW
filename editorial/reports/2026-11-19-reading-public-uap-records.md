# What do the public UAP records establish?

EOTW public-record review · AI-assisted · November 19, 2026

### The claim

A recurring interpretation treats the release of government records about unidentified anomalous phenomena as evidence of an extraterrestrial origin. Two public sources let a reader assess that inference.

### What the archive shows

The National Archives provides access to photographic, audiovisual and written material concerning reported phenomena. Its catalog identifies collections and the agencies that created them. A reader investigating a particular sighting can start with the original item, its date and the account attached to it. [National Archives research guide](https://www.archives.gov/research/topics/uaps)

### What challenges the interpretation

NASA reports that available evidence cannot establish an extraterrestrial origin for UAP. It identifies the shortage of high-quality observations as a limit on the scientific conclusions anyone can draw. A catalog entry alone cannot determine the identity of the object described in it. [NASA UAP study FAQ](https://science.nasa.gov/uap/faqs/)

### What would change the assessment

For an individual case, look for newly released measurements, documented observing conditions and an analysis that other investigators can check. Record the item's identifier so subsequent interpretations can be compared against the same material.
